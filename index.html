🎓 Nina's University - Player Guidance

Game Mechanics

  • 1. The “Continue” button saves your progress every time you return—no need to save manually.
  • 2. Skipping and unseen text are disabled to ensure a full story experience.
  • 3. You cannot go back after reading, but you can check the “History”.
  • 4. Your gender choice affects dialogue, and cannot be changed later.
  • 5. Choices are permanent, so take your time when making decisions!
  • 6. Always quit using the Confirm Exit screen to ensure your progress is saved.
  • 7. For more info or updates, visit the Official Website.

This is a friendly guidance page to help new players understand the game. 💡
